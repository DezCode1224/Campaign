Campaign Priorities

While others remain focused on the politics of the past, Dezmond is focused on the possibilities of our future. He believes leadership should not be defined by old divisions or outdated approaches, but by bold ideas, practical solutions, and a willingness to move communities forward. His vision is rooted in progress, innovation, and collaboration bringing people together to build a stronger, more resilient District 1 where opportunity is accessible, voices are heard, and the next generation can thrive.

Community First

Safeguarding both our heritage and our future. Dezmond is committed to strengthening the Rural Legacy of D1, expanding agricultural preservation, and advancing policies that keep farmland, open space, and natural resources protected for generations to come. He believes responsible leadership also means making sure taxpayer dollars are reinvested locally supporting neighborhood infrastructure, small businesses, schools, and public safety so that the people who live here directly benefit from the resources they contribute.

The Next Generation Guarantee

Dezmond is committed to fostering a true partnership between the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Education to ensure schools have the resources, support, and strategic alignment they need to succeed. His vision includes championing meaningful teacher incentives to attract and retain top educators, strengthening classroom environments, and expanding workforce-linked learning opportunities that connect students to real career pathways. By aligning education with local economic needs, he aims to prepare students not just to graduate, but to thrive in college, careers, and life.

Honoring the Frontline

Dezmond is committed to strengthening recruitment and retention efforts, ensuring competitive compensation, investing in modern equipment and training, and fostering a culture that prioritizes wellness and professional growth. He also believes that supporting public safety families is essential, which is why his approach includes resources and opportunities that uplift spouses and households as a whole. By investing in the people who protect our communities and the families who stand beside them Charles County can build a stronger, safer future for all.

Preserving Our Rural Character

Dezmond supports strong, enforceable Community Benefit Agreements that ensure any large-scale project delivers real value to local communities.Dezmond is committed to responsible planning that prevents overburdened systems and protects public health. His approach prioritizes transparency, accountability, and long-term sustainability so that economic growth never comes at the expense of our neighborhoods, natural resources, or quality of life.

Honest Planning & Healthy Communities

Addressing traffic congestion, eliminating food deserts, and attracting high-quality jobs to Charles County are central to Dezmond’s vision for a stronger, more connected community. He believes residents shouldn’t have to sit in gridlock to get to work, travel miles for fresh groceries, or leave the county to find meaningful career opportunities. That’s why he supports strategic transportation improvements, smart growth planning, and investments that bring essential services closer to where people live.
