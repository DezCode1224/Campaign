The Next Generation Guarantee

Dezmond is committed to fostering a true partnership between the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Education to ensure schools have the resources, support, and strategic alignment they need to succeed. His vision includes championing meaningful teacher incentives to attract and retain top educators, strengthening classroom environments, and expanding workforce-linked learning opportunities that connect students to real career pathways. By aligning education with local economic needs, he aims to prepare students not just to graduate, but to thrive in college, careers, and life.

Connect Education to Careers

Preparing the next generation for success means creating clear, practical pathways from the classroom to meaningful careers. That includes expanding internships, apprenticeships, and job-shadowing opportunities so students can gain real-world experience and explore their interests early. It also means building strong partnerships with local businesses and labor unions to develop career pipelines that connect education directly to employment. By strengthening Career and Technical Education programs aligned with regional workforce needs, we can ensure students graduate not only with diplomas, but with the skills, confidence, and opportunities to thrive in today’s economy.

Guarantee Access to Opportunity

Ensuring every child has a strong start means guaranteeing universal access to high-quality early childhood education and sustained academic support as they grow. That includes investing in after-school programs, tutoring, and summer enrichment opportunities that help students build skills, confidence, and curiosity beyond the classroom. It also means expanding reliable broadband access so every student regardless of zip code has the tools they need to learn, connect, and compete on equal footing in today’s digital world. /p>

Prioritize Student Well-Being

Supporting student success means caring for the whole child, not just their academics. That requires expanding access to mental health services in schools so students have the support they need to navigate challenges and thrive. It also means investing in strong nutrition programs and safe, reliable transportation to ensure every child is healthy and ready to learn. Equally important is creating mentorship and leadership opportunities that empower young people, build confidence, and help them develop the skills to become the next generation of community leaders.
