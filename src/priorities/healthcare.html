Community First

Safeguarding both our heritage and our future. Dezmond is committed to strengthening the Rural Legacy of D1, expanding agricultural preservation, and advancing policies that keep farmland, open space, and natural resources protected for generations to come. He believes responsible leadership also means making sure taxpayer dollars are reinvested locally supporting neighborhood infrastructure, small businesses, schools, and public safety so that the people who live here directly benefit from the resources they contribute.

Invest Locally

Reinvesting tax dollars back into the community means strengthening neighborhood infrastructure, improving schools, enhancing public safety, and ensuring local services meet residents’ needs. It also means supporting small businesses through targeted grants, fair procurement opportunities, and hands. on technical assistance so they can grow and thrive. Just as importantly, it means prioritizing local hiring for public projects—creating jobs for residents and ensuring that the economic benefits of public investment stay within the community.

Listen Before Leading

Putting community first requires consistent engagement and meaningful opportunities for residents to be heard. That means holding regular town halls, listening tours, and community forums where people can share their concerns and ideas directly. It also means establishing advisory councils that reflect the full diversity of the community—including youth, seniors, workers, and small business owners—so decisions are informed by real perspectives.

Protect What Matters Most

Protecting what matters most means safeguarding the spaces and systems that sustain our quality of life. That includes preserving green space, farmland, and historic sites that define our community’s character and heritage. It also means strengthening public safety through proactive prevention, youth engagement, and strong community partnerships, while ensuring residents can rely on safe housing, clean streets, and dependable transportation every day.
